[Intro: instrumental, traditional instruments are joined by electric guitar, 8 bars]
[Verse 1]
I washed up on a stranger's shore with nothing but my name,
they gave me clothes to wear, and they were glad enough I came;
I've washed up on a thousand shores, and every one was new,
and every time they greet me, then they wonder: who are you?
[Chorus 1]
Who are you, and where's your home, and who's your family?
Nobody, I tell them. I'm nobody. That's me.
But I'm the one of many turns, and I'll take my turn a gain,
till they take the stick I carry for a fan to winnow grain.
[Verse 2]
In halls I've never entered, they are singing of my name,
and not all of it was me, but they sing it just the same;
I've been cursed for things I did, and things that no-one did at all,
and the ones who rowed the hardest aren't remembered in the hall.
[Verse 3]
I've sat with kings and generals, and listened to their war;
I've heard the song that knows it all, and all that's gone before;
I've told a hundred stories, and I've told them all as mine,
and some were so like the truth, I couldn't tell you where's the line.
[Chorus 2]
Who are you, and where's your home, and who's your family?
Nobody, I tell them, and there's plenty who agree.
I'm the one of many crafts, and I'll turn my hand a gain,
till they take the weight I carry for a fan to winnow grain.
[Verse 4]
I've gone down among the dead, and they told me what they knew;
ask me what they told me, and I'll tell it back to you;
but it's all that I can carry back, the words and not the dead,
and the first of them was one of mine, and this is what he said:
[Bridge: quiet, drums drop out, a second male voice]
I fell asleep before the dawn, and fell, and no one knew;
I don't ask to come back home, I only ask of you
an oar stood up above me in the sand, out in the air,
so nobody sailing past can say that nobody was there.
[Lead, quiet]
So I went back, and found him, and I raised him up a mound,
and I stood his oar above him, and I set it in the ground.
[Instrumental break]
[Verse 5]
And the powers in their palaces have quarrelled over me:
some have fought to bring me home, and some have cursed me bitterly;
they kept me safely on an island where no ship could come or go,
till word came down from up on high that they must let me go.
[Chorus 3]
Who are you, and where's your home, and who's your family?
Nobody, I tell them, and there's plenty who agree.
I'm the one of many ways, and they all lead home a gain,
till they take the oar I carry for a fan to winnow grain.
[Verse 6]
You won't know me at the doorway, for I'm not like what you've known,
and you'd be right to test me first, and not take my word alone.
[Female Vocal]
So I lied to you to test you, and I watched you while I lied:
said the tree you'd built your life around was cut and moved aside.
[Male Vocal]
But no one moves that tree, for it is rooted far and wide
in everyone who ever lived, and wrote, and loved, and died,
and it branches out a thousand ways, and every branch is new.
[Female Vocal]
And you knew it by its roots, and so I knew that you were true.
[Chorus 4]
Who are you, and where's your home, and who's your family?
[Male alone]
Nobody, I tell them. I'm nobody. Just me.
I'm the one of many turns, and I'll take my turn a gain,
till they take the oar I carry for a fan to winnow grain.
[Verse 7: Male Vocal, building]
There's a prophecy they tell me, and I hope that it is so:
that I'll shoulder up the oar that brought me through the storms, and go
till someday I reach a people who have never faced the sea,
who've never learned to fear it, and have never heard of me.
[Faster]
So I'll walk till they call it a winnowing fan,
till it's only a tool in a farmer's hand,
[F:]till nobody's frightened of the thing I bore,
and I'll plant it in the ground, and face the sea no more.
[M:]Not a throne at the end of the road, not a crown,
just a field, and a harvest, and a place to set it down;
[F:]and maybe the oar will take root in the ground,
and branch like the tree my whole life was built round,
[M:]and the ones who went before me will be sitting in its shade,
and we'll talk about the voyage, and the songs, and what we made.
[Outro: slow, everyone]
And nobody sailing past will think that nobody was there.
[Female]
And nobody sailing past will think that nobody was there.
[End]
